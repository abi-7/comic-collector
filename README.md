# 📚 Comic Collector

A mobile comic book collection manager that lets you scan barcodes to instantly add comics to your digital library and access detailed information about each book.

<h1>🎯 Features</h1>

📱 Scan comic book barcodes using your phone camera <br>
📖 Automatically fetch and display comic details <br>
📊 Organize and track your entire comic collection <br>
📲 Native mobile experience (iOS & Android support) <br>
🎨 Clean, modern interface designed for collectors <br>

<h1>🚀 Usage</h1>

1. Open the app and tap the scan button to activate your camera. <br>
2. Point your camera at a comic book barcode. <br>
3. The app automatically reads the barcode and adds the comic to your collection. <br>
4. Browse your collection and tap any comic to view full details. <br>
5. Keep track of all your comics in one organized place! <br>
